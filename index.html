Deploytual

Deploy intelligence. Any data. Anywhere.

PROBLEM

Organizations struggle to turn heterogeneous data into analysis without manually writing pipelines, SQL, or ML code. Analysts and engineers waste significant time stitching tools together instead of answering business questions.

ENGINEERING

  • • Natural Language → Executable Analysis: Converts plain-English questions into Pandas operations or SQL, then executes them against uploaded or connected data sources.
  • • End-to-End Pipeline Orchestration: Chains data cleaning → anomaly detection (Isolation Forest) → forecasting (Prophet) → executive PDF generation in a single workflow.
  • • Explainable AI Layer: Every answer surfaces the exact code/SQL used, plus text-to-speech narration for accessibility.
  • • Production Deployment: Fully Dockerized with CI/CD. Supports Kubernetes + Helm. Live on Render (API) and Netlify (frontend).

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

  • • Why FastAPI? Async-friendly, automatic OpenAPI docs, and excellent for building agent-facing APIs quickly.
  • • Why container-first? The goal was “deploy anywhere” — Docker + Helm makes the system portable across client environments.
  • • Why expose the generated code? Trust and debuggability matter more than black-box answers in analytics tools.

02 / WORKFORCE ANALYTICS PLATFORM
Jan 2026 – May 2026

WorkforceIQ

From raw data to predictive models to dashboards people actually open.

PROBLEM

HR teams typically have attrition data, engagement scores, and call transcripts living in separate systems. By the time patterns are noticed, good employees have already left.

WHAT WAS BUILT

  • • End-to-End Pipeline: Ingested and scored 50K+ employee records and 10K call transcripts into a reusable analytics backbone.
  • • Attrition Model: Random Forest classifier achieving 85%+ accuracy. Prioritized recall and F1 because attrition is class-imbalanced — missing a high-risk employee is more costly than a false positive.
  • • Behavioral Segmentation: K-Means clustering revealed a small high-risk, low-satisfaction cohort that traditional reporting missed.
  • • Sentiment Layer: VADER analysis on call transcripts with ~92% label agreement, feeding real-time sentiment signals into dashboards.
  • • Delivery: Tableau + self-contained Plotly dashboards + Power Automate email workflows that cut manual reporting time by ~60%.

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

  • • Why Random Forest over deeper models? Feature importance was more valuable than marginal accuracy gains. HR needed to understand *why* someone was flagged.
  • • Why both Tableau and Plotly? Tableau for stakeholders already in that ecosystem; Plotly HTML for zero-install sharing.
