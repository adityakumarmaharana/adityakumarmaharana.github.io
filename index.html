02 / WORKFORCE ANALYTICSJan 2026 – May 2026

WorkforceIQ

From raw data to predictive models to dashboards people actually open.

PROBLEM

Attrition data, engagement scores, and call transcripts live in separate systems. By the time patterns are noticed, good employees have already left.

WHAT WAS BUILT

  • •End-to-end pipeline: ingested and scored 50K+ employee records and 10K call transcripts into a reusable analytics backbone.
  • •Attrition model: Random Forest at 85%+ accuracy, tuned for recall and F1 because missing a high-risk employee costs more than a false positive.
  • •Behavioral segmentation: K-Means surfaced a high-risk, low-satisfaction cohort invisible to standard reporting.
  • •Sentiment layer: VADER analysis on transcripts with ~92% label agreement feeding live dashboard signals.
  • •Delivery: Tableau + self-contained Plotly dashboards + Power Automate workflows that cut manual reporting time ~60%.

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

  • •Why Random Forest? Feature importance beat marginal accuracy gains — stakeholders needed to know why someone was flagged.
  • •Why Tableau and Plotly? Tableau for existing stakeholders; Plotly HTML for zero-install sharing.
