02 / WORKFORCE ANALYTICS
Jan 2026 – May 2026

WorkforceIQ

From raw data to predictive models to dashboards people actually open.

PROBLEM

Attrition data, engagement scores, and call transcripts usually live in separate systems. By the time patterns are noticed, good employees have already left.

WHAT WAS BUILT

  • •End-to-end pipeline: Ingested and scored 50K+ employee records and 10K call transcripts into a reusable analytics backbone.
  • •Attrition model: Random Forest achieving 85%+ accuracy. Prioritized recall and F1 because attrition is class-imbalanced — missing a high-risk employee costs more than a false positive.
  • •Behavioral segmentation: K-Means revealed a small high-risk, low-satisfaction cohort invisible to standard reporting.
  • •Sentiment layer: VADER analysis on transcripts with ~92% label agreement, feeding live signals into dashboards.
  • •Delivery: Tableau + self-contained Plotly dashboards + Power Automate workflows that reduced manual reporting time by ~60%.

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

  • •Why Random Forest? Feature importance was more valuable than marginal accuracy gains. Stakeholders needed to understand why someone was flagged.
  • •Why both Tableau and Plotly? Tableau for existing stakeholders; Plotly HTML for zero-install sharing.
